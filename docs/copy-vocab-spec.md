## Verbs
Use: own · build · set · drive · shape · scale · test · codify · kill (as "cut") · translate · turn … into · surface · direct · evaluate · brief · benchmark · document · partner with · hand over.
Avoid: responsible for · participated in · involved in · helped (as the main verb) · supported (as the main verb) · worked on · successfully · various · multiple · improved efficiency.
Ownership boundary (honest + senior): "Owned X; Y was led by a dedicated Z" — never "helped with X".

## Nouns
creative angles · hooks · scripts · briefs · creative testing · experimentation framework · testing roadmap · playbook · brief templates · creator roster / creator lineup · UGC · creator content · paid amplification · brand creative vs UGC · funnel · acquisition · benchmark · winners · learnings.
Cross-industry: say **users / installs / audience** outside a case; "players" only inside a Genshin/ZZZ case where it is literal.

## Metrics
Use freely (she has the evidence): CPM · CTR · CPC · CPI · LTV · ROI · engagement rate · views · installs · redemptions · spend share.
Use only with care: ROAS · CAC · MER · payback (DTC/subscription terms; F2P maths differs; fine to read, don't claim).
Format: × for multiples (3.7×), % with sign (+338%), "vs benchmark" for every comparison; before → after where possible (1% → 15%). JDs now ask for before/after proof.

## Sentence patterns JDs reward
1. Turn / translate X into Y. — "turned creator videos into measurable UA performance"
2. …, not just … — "drove installs, not just views"
3. Scale what works, cut what doesn't, document the learnings.
4. Use data to guide decisions, not simply report results.
5. State change, not action: "Brand creative went from 1% to 15% of UA spend" beats "I increased…"

## House rules (from CLAUDE.md + LESSONS, kept)
Native English only; British spelling (optimise, localised, analyse, programme); first person; concise; no game version numbers; Results ≤45 chars, lowercase label; What I did = "Keyword phrase: one strategic decision + evidence"; no process detail (counts, cadences) in What I did; no "side hustle"; "the Genshin Impact brand team", never "my team"; one meaning per phrase per case (no repeats); no internal terms ("matrix", "third-party community", "Brand-to-UA") without plain words.
